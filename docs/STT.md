## Things to know

- **Send WAV, not FLAC.** A FLAC upload gets no answer at all from any of these models (no error, the request just hangs; tested 2026-11-18 on all six, 60 s each). The same clip as 16 kHz mono WAV comes back in about a second. Convert first, e.g. `ffmpeg -i in.flac -ac 1 -ar 16000 out.wav`.
- **`openai/Whisper-large-v3-turbo` sometimes adds words that aren't there:** 6 of 73 clips ended in a made-up "Thank you. Thank you. Thank you." (a known Whisper habit on the silence at the end of a clip); that accounts for part of its error rate. Trim trailing silence or check the end of its transcripts.
- Some models occasionally run two words together where sentences join ("burkettFoster"), counted as errors here.

Clean, read English is the easy case: expect higher error rates on noisy audio, accents, several speakers or other languages. Run it on your own Tiiny with `python tools/stt_bench.py` (needs ffmpeg).
